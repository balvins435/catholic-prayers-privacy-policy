Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-02

Catholic Prayers is an offline-first Catholic prayer and devotional reference app. It includes a bundled offline Bible, daily readings, prayers, saints and novenas, locally saved favorites and prayer progress, optional local prayer reminders, advertising, and crash/performance diagnostics. The app does not require an account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

Information Processed by the App

Information stored only on the device

Favorites, prayer-view history, daily prayer streaks, Rosary progress, novena progress, Bible reading position and bookmarks, reading preferences, reminder settings, and any ad-free time earned by watching a rewarded ad are stored locally on the device. Catholic Prayers does not upload this information to an app-owned server.

If a user enables a prayer reminder, the app requests notification permission and schedules a local notification on the device. The reminder may use a bundled bell sound. Notification permission and sound behavior can be changed in the device settings.

Offline Bible and daily readings

The Bible, daily verses, daily liturgical readings, prayers, saints, and novenas are read from data bundled with the app. Reading them does not send a request to USCCB or any other external reading website.

Advertising

The app shows banner, native, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Some builds may request ads through the TopOn (ThinkUp) mediation SDK, which can request ads from Google and other advertising partners.

These SDKs may automatically collect and share information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. They use it to show ads, measure ad performance, limit how often ads repeat, and prevent fraud. An IP address may be used to estimate a device's general location.

Ads may be personalized, meaning chosen using information about a user's activity across other apps and websites, where the user has allowed it:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app asks for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. Users who do not consent receive non-personalized or limited ads, which are chosen using context such as the app's content and general location rather than a user's past activity.
  • Users can review or change that choice at any time with Advertising privacy choices in the app's Settings screen, where it is shown.
  • On Android, users can reset or delete their advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in the device's Google or privacy settings.

Ads are not targeted based on a user's religious beliefs.

Crash and performance diagnostics

Release builds use Sentry to receive crash reports, error details, app release information, device and operating-system information, session health, sampled navigation and performance measurements, and diagnostic breadcrumbs such as screen changes and app log messages leading up to an error. Sentry's collection of default personally identifiable information is disabled.

How Information Is Used and Shared

Catholic Prayers does not sell personal data. Information collected by the advertising and diagnostics SDKs described above is processed by those providers to deliver ads, measure reliability and advertising performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with applicable law. Catholic Prayers does not operate an account database or advertising profile database of its own.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app information remains on the device until it is cleared by the user, removed through device settings, or deleted when the app is uninstalled. Catholic Prayers has no app-owned account or cloud record to delete. Information processed by Google, TopOn and its advertising partners, or Sentry is retained and deleted under those providers' policies and user-control processes.

Data Security

The app's network connections use HTTPS; Android blocks unencrypted (cleartext) traffic for this app by default. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but the app limits its own collection and keeps prayer preferences and progress on the device.

Children's Privacy

Catholic Prayers is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not designed as a child-directed service. The app does not knowingly request personal information from children. If the app's intended audience changes, its advertising, consent, store declarations, and privacy practices will be reviewed before that change is published.

Religious Content and Affiliation

Catholic Prayers is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by USCCB, the Holy See, any diocese, parish, religious order, or other religious organization unless separately stated in an official listing.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
